Therapist: How has it felt to take a break during the workday?
Client: At first I worried that leaving my desk would make me fall behind. On Tuesday I took a short walk near the office. I noticed, "I can pause and still get things done."
Therapist: What helped you notice that?
Client: I came back more settled and finished writing the email I had been putting off. My colleague Morgan had mentioned taking breaks, but it felt different to try it myself.
Therapist: We looked at the thought that a break would make you fall behind and compared it with what happened on Tuesday.
Client: That made it easier to see the difference. I still found it harder to step away on days with back-to-back meetings.
Therapist: What was different on the days you could step away?
Client: I had more room in my schedule and I could choose when to leave my desk. I felt less rushed when I came back.
